# Combined Terminal

## Purpose

The Combined Terminal is a Training Probe adapter that lets an analog meter and VAMeter-Edu be used together without making the classroom circuit visually complex.

## Public technical description

- Banana plug: stacks on the analog-meter terminal.
- Banana jack: continues the classroom circuit connection.
- Two-core cable: connects the terminal pair to VAMeter-Edu through RCA pin plugs.
- Cable gland: provides strain relief.

## Manufacturing notes

- Print positive-side terminal in red and negative-side terminal in black when possible.
- Heat-set M5 insert nut for banana plug mounting.
- Heat-set M8 insert nut for banana jack mounting.
- Solder the two-core cable to the banana plug and banana jack.
- Fix the cable with a cable gland.

## Wiring caution

RCA jacks are mounted on the VAMeter-Edu case side.
The Combined Terminal is connected to the case-side RCA jacks through RCA pin plugs at the cable end.
Do not describe the Combined Terminal as containing an RCA jack.

## Verification

Record continuity check results before classroom use:

- no unintended short between cable cores
- expected continuity for each conductor
- no loose plug/jack after repeated insertion
- cable gland provides strain relief
